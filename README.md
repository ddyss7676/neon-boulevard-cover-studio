Neon Boulevard Cover Studio turns your photos and a caption into GTA-style cover art — multi-panel posters with bold lettering and a Vice City sunset / neon colour grade — and exports them as PNGs to share. It runs entirely offline in the browser; photos never leave your machine.

- 8 layouts (hero left/right/top, four-grid, triptych, full bleed, hero + strip, split frame)
- 6 colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Hour, Teal Dusk, Hot Magenta, Mono Noir)
- Up to 5 photos, per-panel crop: click a panel to select it, drag to pan, scroll to zoom, or use the sliders (zoom, pan, rotate)
- Caption, tagline and badge text with live preview; canvas-drawn palms, sun and starburst badge
- One-click PNG export in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080 — or all three at once

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. No build step, no network request, no install.
